Motivation

Modern 4th-generation X-ray facilities provide unprecedented opportunities for probing ultrafast dynamics. Their exceptionally high photon flux per pulse, combined with ultra-short pulse duration and megahertz repetition rates, enables ultra-fast single-shot imaging of stochastic phenomena in matter.
