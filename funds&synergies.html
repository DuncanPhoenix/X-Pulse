Motivation




Modern 4th-generation X-ray facilities provide unprecedented opportunities for probing ultrafast dynamics. Their exceptionally high photon flux per pulse (109 - 1012 photons per pulse), combined with ultra-short pulse duration (~10 fs and ~100 ps at XFEL’s and synchrotrons) with pulse megahertz repetition rates, enables ultra-fast single-shot/single exposure imaging of stochastic phenomena in matter.

Funding and Project Synergies


This project builds on and complements several national, international, and European initiatives:

# Initiative
1Röntgen Ångström Cluster, InVision, 2019–2024. PIs: John Banhard (TU Berlin), Stephen Hall (Lund University), Patrik Vagovič (work package leader at EuXFEL).
2European XFEL R&D project: MHz X-ray microscopy (2020–2022). PI: Patrik Vagovič.
3HORIZON-EIC Pathfinder (2022–2025), Grant No. 101046448. PI: Patrik Vagovič. https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-pathfinder_en
4Slovak Technological and Innovation Consortium project, “Stephanik” (2025–2027).
5STFC International pipeline project (application submitted) to support X-PULSE activities at European XFEL. https://www.xfel.eu/

Access and funding at P25 will leverage these investments, creating a platform with high international visibility and strong user engagement. We are preparing to propose a user consortium to the European XFEL management, where our instruments will be installed at SASE1, and a collaboration with ESRF ID19 on fast imaging is already established. The fast-imaging capabilities at P25 will significantly enhance Europe’s existing strengths in this rapidly growing field by expanding access to advanced fast and ultrafast X-ray imaging.
