Motivation




Modern 4th-generation X-ray facilities provide unprecedented opportunities for probing ultrafast dynamics. Their exceptionally high photon flux per pulse (109 - 1012 photons per pulse), combined with ultra-short pulse duration (~10 fs and ~100 ps at XFEL’s and synchrotrons) with pulse megahertz repetition rates, enables ultra-fast single-shot/single exposure imaging of stochastic phenomena in matter.

X-Pulse

X-ray Probing of Ultrafast Events

Our mission is to push boundaries of advanced X-ray pulse resolved imaging at large scale X-ray infrastructures.



X-PULSE is a dynasty of leading scientists in the area of ultra-fast imaging. We thrive on creating emerging methodologies that shed light on the world's tiniest mysteries.


Why do we do it?

First of all, it’s fun. But we also see the leaps and bounds of enabling progress in the fields of medicine and materials science.


How do we do it?

We are sort of filmmakers, but the hilarious thing is we have no control over the action. We create the perfect scene, trigger the camera roll, and "ACTION!" ... and wait for the elements to show us their play. We record it and rejoice over being the first ever audience to see the ultrafine movie.
